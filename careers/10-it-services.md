- [Accenture](https://www.accenture.com/us-en/careers) — consulting and delivery across every industry.
- [Deloitte](https://careers.deloitte.com/) — technology consulting, audit and risk advisory.
- [Capgemini](https://www.capgemini.com/careers/) — engineering and digital transformation services.
- [Cognizant](https://careers.cognizant.com/) — large India delivery centres and steady fresher hiring.
- [Tata Consultancy Services](https://www.tcs.com/careers) — the biggest Indian IT employer; hires through NQT.
- [Infosys](https://career.infosys.com/) — Mysore training campus and the HackWithInfy hiring route.
- [Wipro](https://careers.wipro.com/) — services and engineering; hires through the Elite NTH test.
- [HCLTech](https://www.hcltech.com/careers) — engineering and R&D services; also hires straight after class 12.
- [DXC Technology](https://careers.dxc.com/) — enterprise IT operations and application management.
- [Kyndryl](https://www.kyndryl.com/us/en/careers) — the infrastructure services business spun out of IBM.
- [Thoughtworks](https://www.thoughtworks.com/careers) — smaller, craft-focused consultancy; strong engineering practices.
- [Concentrix](https://jobs.concentrix.com/) — customer experience technology and support operations.
- [Insight Enterprises](https://careers.insight.com/) — IT solutions integration and cloud services.
- [CDW](https://www.cdwjobs.com/) — technology procurement and managed services.
- [Leidos](https://careers.leidos.com/) — defence, intelligence and health IT; US clearance often required.
- [SAIC](https://www.saic.com/careers) — government systems integration and mission IT.
- [Booz Allen Hamilton](https://careers.boozallen.com/) — consulting and analytics for US government agencies.
- [Unisys](https://www.unisys.com/careers/) — mainframe modernisation and managed infrastructure.
