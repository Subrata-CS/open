---
title: Big tech
about: The largest employers of software engineers on earth. Huge intakes, structured interviews, published levels.
order: 1
---

- [Google](https://careers.google.com/) — search, Android, Cloud, and the biggest new-grad intake anywhere.
- [Microsoft](https://careers.microsoft.com/) — Azure, Windows, Office, GitHub and OpenAI money.
- [Apple](https://jobs.apple.com/) — silicon, iOS and hardware; teams are secretive by design.
- [Amazon](https://amazon.jobs/) — AWS plus retail; the largest volume of software roles in the world.
- [Meta](https://www.metacareers.com/) — Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, PyTorch and Llama.
- [Netflix](https://jobs.netflix.com/) — small teams, senior-only culture, top of the market pay.
- [NVIDIA](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/about-nvidia/careers/) — GPUs, CUDA and the hardware the whole AI industry runs on.
- [Intel](https://jobs.intel.com/) — x86 CPUs, foundry, compilers and firmware.
- [AMD](https://careers.amd.com/) — Ryzen, EPYC and ROCm; strong for computer architecture.
- [Qualcomm](https://www.qualcomm.com/company/careers) — mobile SoCs and modems; huge India engineering presence.
- [IBM](https://www.ibm.com/careers) — mainframes, consulting, Red Hat and quantum computing.
- [Oracle](https://careers.oracle.com/) — databases, OCI cloud and enterprise applications.
- [Cisco](https://jobs.cisco.com/) — networking, Webex and security; strong systems engineering.
- [Adobe](https://careers.adobe.com/) — Creative Cloud, PDF and Firefly; graphics and ML roles.
- [Salesforce](https://careers.salesforce.com/) — the largest SaaS company; Slack, Tableau and MuleSoft too.
- [SAP](https://jobs.sap.com/) — enterprise resource planning; huge Germany and India centres.
- [Tesla](https://www.tesla.com/careers) — autonomy, embedded firmware, energy and manufacturing software.
- [Samsung](https://www.samsung.com/us/careers/) — phones, displays and memory; R&D centres across Asia.
- [Sony](https://www.sony.com/en/SonyInfo/Careers/) — PlayStation, imaging sensors and entertainment technology.
- [LG Electronics](https://www.lg.com/global/careers) — consumer electronics, displays and webOS.
