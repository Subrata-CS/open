- [Broadcom](https://www.broadcom.com/company/careers) — networking silicon plus the VMware and CA software portfolio.
- [Micron](https://careers.micron.com/) — DRAM and NAND memory; fabs in the US, Japan and India.
- [Texas Instruments](https://careers.ti.com/) — analog and embedded processors; strong new-graduate programme.
- [Analog Devices](https://careers.analog.com/) — signal processing, converters and power management.
- [Applied Materials](https://careers.appliedmaterials.com/) — the machines that make chips; process and software roles.
- [ASML](https://www.asml.com/en/careers) — EUV lithography; the hardest optics and control problem in tech.
- [KLA](https://careers.kla.com/) — process control and wafer inspection; heavy computer vision.
- [Lam Research](https://careers.lamresearch.com/) — etch and deposition equipment for fabs.
- [Marvell](https://www.marvell.com/company/careers.html) — data infrastructure silicon; custom AI and networking chips.
- [Infineon](https://www.infineon.com/cms/en/careers/) — power semiconductors, automotive and security chips.
- [NXP Semiconductors](https://www.nxp.com/company/about-nxp/careers:CAREERS) — automotive, edge processing and secure connectivity.
- [onsemi](https://www.onsemi.com/careers) — power and sensing silicon for automotive and industry.
- [Renesas Electronics](https://www.renesas.com/en/about/careers) — microcontrollers and automotive systems-on-chip.
- [Synopsys](https://careers.synopsys.com/) — EDA tools and IP; where chip design software is written.
- [Arm](https://careers.arm.com/) — the instruction set in every phone; architecture and compilers.
- [TSMC](https://careers.tsmc.com/) — the foundry that fabricates most advanced chips on earth.
