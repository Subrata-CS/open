- [PayPal](https://careers.pypl.com/) — payments, Venmo and Braintree; heavy risk and fraud modelling.
- [Stripe](https://stripe.com/jobs) — developer-first payments; famously high engineering bar.
- [Block](https://block.xyz/careers) — Square, Cash App and Bitcoin infrastructure.
- [Visa](https://jobs.visa.com/) — the card network itself; extreme reliability requirements.
- [Klarna](https://www.klarna.com/careers/) — buy now pay later; Swedish fintech with a big AI push.
- [Revolut](https://www.revolut.com/careers/) — digital banking across Europe; fast, demanding culture.
- [Airbnb](https://careers.airbnb.com/) — marketplace, search ranking and trust systems.
- [Uber](https://www.uber.com/careers/) — real-time dispatch, mapping and pricing at global scale.
- [DoorDash](https://careers.doordash.com/) — logistics, routing and marketplace optimisation.
- [Expedia Group](https://careers.expediagroup.com/) — travel search, pricing and inventory systems.
- [eBay](https://jobs.ebayinc.com/) — one of the oldest marketplaces; search and recommendation work.
- [Wayfair](https://www.aboutwayfair.com/careers) — e-commerce, supply chain and 3D product visualisation.
- [GoDaddy](https://careers.godaddy.com/) — domains, hosting and small-business web tooling.
- [Indeed](https://www.indeed.jobs/) — job search itself; large-scale matching and ranking.
- [LinkedIn](https://careers.linkedin.com/) — professional graph, feed ranking and Kafka's birthplace.
- [Pinterest](https://www.pinterestcareers.com/) — visual discovery; computer vision and recommendations.
- [Discord](https://discord.com/careers) — real-time chat and voice for hundreds of millions of users.
- [Spotify](https://www.lifeatspotify.com/jobs) — audio streaming, recommendations and a strong engineering culture.
- [Bloomberg](https://careers.bloomberg.com/) — the Terminal; low-latency market data and C++ at scale.
- [Yahoo](https://www.yahooinc.com/careers/) — search, mail, finance and advertising technology.
- [Yandex](https://yandex.com/jobs) — search, maps and self-driving research.
- [Xero](https://www.xero.com/about/careers/) — cloud accounting for small business; New Zealand and Australia.
