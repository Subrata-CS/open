title: Cloud, software and SaaS
about: Product companies selling software to other companies. Steady hiring, remote-friendly, real engineering depth.

- [Atlassian](https://www.atlassian.com/company/careers) — Jira, Confluence and Bitbucket; distributed by default.
- [ServiceNow](https://careers.servicenow.com/) — enterprise workflow platform; large India and US teams.
- [Workday](https://www.workday.com/en-us/company/careers.html) — HR and finance cloud; the ATS half the industry applies through.
- [Shopify](https://www.shopify.com/careers) — commerce at scale, Ruby and Rails; remote-first.
- [Dropbox](https://jobs.dropbox.com/) — file sync and storage; strong distributed systems work.
- [Box](https://www.box.com/about-us/careers) — enterprise content management and secure sharing.
- [Zoom](https://careers.zoom.us/) — real-time video, media codecs and low-latency networking.
- [Zendesk](https://www.zendesk.com/jobs/) — customer support software; product and platform roles.
- [Intuit](https://www.intuit.com/careers/) — TurboTax, QuickBooks and Mailchimp; applied ML on financial data.
- [Autodesk](https://www.autodesk.com/careers) — CAD, geometry and simulation; graphics-heavy engineering.
- [Twilio](https://www.twilio.com/en-us/company/jobs) — messaging and voice APIs; remote-friendly.
- [RingCentral](https://www.ringcentral.com/careers.html) — cloud communications and contact centre software.
- [OpenText](https://careers.opentext.com/) — information management; acquires and runs many enterprise products.
- [Citrix](https://careers.cloud.com/) — virtual desktops and delivery, now part of Cloud Software Group.
- [Veeam](https://careers.veeam.com/) — backup and data resilience for virtual and cloud workloads.
- [Veritas](https://www.veritas.com/company/careers) — enterprise data protection and storage management.
- [DigitalOcean](https://www.digitalocean.com/careers) — developer-friendly cloud; smaller and less bureaucratic than the big three.
- [Canonical](https://canonical.com/careers) — Ubuntu; fully remote, written interviews, open source throughout.
- [Red Hat](https://www.redhat.com/en/jobs) — enterprise Linux, Kubernetes and OpenShift; open source by contract.
- [VMware](https://www.broadcom.com/company/careers) — virtualisation and Tanzu, now hiring under Broadcom.
- [Alteryx](https://www.alteryx.com/about-us/careers) — self-service analytics and data preparation.
- [Tableau](https://careers.salesforce.com/) — data visualisation; roles are posted through Salesforce.
- [ADP](https://jobs.adp.com/) — payroll and HR at enormous scale; steady, stable engineering.
- [Bentley Systems](https://www.bentley.com/careers/) — infrastructure engineering software and digital twins.
- [Avaya](https://www.avaya.com/en/about-avaya/careers/) — business communications and contact centre platforms.
- [Rackspace](https://www.rackspace.com/careers) — managed multicloud services and support engineering.
- [Splunk](https://www.splunk.com/en_us/careers.html) — log search and observability, now part of Cisco.
