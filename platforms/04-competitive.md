---
title: Competitive programming
about: Timed contests against everyone else. The fastest way to find your gaps.
order: 4
---

- [Codeforces](https://codeforces.com/) — the main arena. Rated contests several times a month.
- [AtCoder](https://atcoder.jp/) — famously well-written problems and a beginner contest series.
- [CodeChef](https://www.codechef.com/) — long and short contests, strong Indian community.
- [HackerEarth](https://www.hackerearth.com/) — contests plus hiring challenges.
- [Topcoder](https://www.topcoder.com/) — the oldest of them, with algorithm marathons.
- [ICPC](https://icpc.global/) — the university team championship. Worth aiming at.
