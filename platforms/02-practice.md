title: Programming practice
about: Reps. The point is volume and variety, not difficulty.
order: 2
---

- [HackerRank](https://www.hackerrank.com/) — structured tracks by language and topic, with a gentle curve.
- [Codewars](https://www.codewars.com/) — short katas ranked by difficulty; you see other solutions after yours.
- [GeeksforGeeks](https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/) — enormous archive of explained problems and articles.
- [CodingBat](https://codingbat.com/) — tiny Java and Python drills, ideal for the first month.
- [Coderbyte](https://coderbyte.com/) — challenges plus interview preparation material.
- [Kattis](https://open.kattis.com/) — a clean judge with problems from real contests.
