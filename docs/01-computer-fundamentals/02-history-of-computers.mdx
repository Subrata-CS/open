# History of Computers

<Lede>
The history of computers is the journey from simple manual calculating tools to today's intelligent AI-powered machines. Every invention contributed to the modern computer we use today.

## What is the History of Computers?

<Callout kind="def" title="Definition">
The history of computers refers to the chronological development of calculating devices and computer systems from ancient times to the modern digital era.
</Callout>

### Why is it Important?

- Understand technological evolution
- Learn about famous inventors
- Understand computer generations
- Know how modern computers were developed

# 1. Abacus

![Abacus](/img/abacus.jpg)

*Figure 2. Ancient Abacus*

The **Abacus** was the world's first calculating device. It used beads placed on rods to perform arithmetic operations.

### Features

| Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| Invented | Around 2400 BC |
| Type | Manual Calculator |
| Operations | Addition & Subtraction |
| Power | No Electricity |

---

# 2. Napier's Bones

![Napier](/img/napier-bones.jpg)

*Figure 3. Napier's Bones*

Invented by **John Napier** in 1617.

### Uses

- Multiplication
- Division
- Square Roots

---

# 3. Pascaline

![Pascaline](/img/pascaline.jpg)

*Figure 4. Mechanical Calculator*

The **Pascaline** was invented by **Blaise Pascal** and was the first successful mechanical calculator.

| Feature | Value |
|---|---|
| Year | 1642 |
| Inventor | Blaise Pascal |
| Technology | Mechanical Gears |

---

# 4. Difference Engine

![Difference Engine](/img/difference-engine.jpg)

*Figure 5. Difference Engine*

Designed by **Charles Babbage** for automatic mathematical calculations.

<Callout kind="tip" title="Exam Fact">
Charles Babbage is called the **Father of Computer** because he introduced the concept of programmable computing.
</Callout>

---

# 5. Analytical Engine

![Analytical Engine](/img/analytical-engine.jpg)

*Figure 6. Analytical Engine*

The Analytical Engine was the first design of a **programmable computer**.

| Analytical Engine | Modern Computer |
|---|---|
| Mill | CPU |
| Store | Memory |
| Punch Cards | Input |
| Printer | Output |

---

# Ada Lovelace

![Ada Lovelace](/img/ada-lovelace.jpg)

*Figure 7. Ada Lovelace*

Ada Lovelace wrote the first algorithm for the Analytical Engine and is known as the **First Computer Programmer**.

---

# Harvard Mark I

![Mark I](/img/mark1.jpg)

*Figure 8. Harvard Mark I*

An electromechanical computer developed in 1944 by **Howard Aiken** with IBM.

---

# ENIAC

![ENIAC](/img/eniac.jpg)

*Figure 9. ENIAC Computer*

ENIAC stands for **Electronic Numerical Integrator and Computer**.

| Feature | ENIAC |
|---|---|
| Weight | 30 Tons |
| Vacuum Tubes | 18,000 |
| Year | 1946 |
