# What is the History of Computers?

Computer history describes the development of calculating devices from ancient manual tools to modern electronic digital computers.

### Why Study History?

- Understand technological evolution
- Learn about famous inventors
- Understand computer generations
- Know how modern computers were developed

# 1. Abacus (2400 BC)

![Abacus](/img/abacus.jpg)

*Figure 2. Ancient Abacus*

The **Abacus** is considered the world's first calculating device.

### Features

- Wooden frame
- Beads on rods
- Manual calculation
- Used for addition & subtraction

### Advantages

- Simple
- No electricity
- Fast manual calculation

### Limitation

Cannot store data.

---

# 2. Napier's Bones (1617)

![Napier Bones](/img/napier-bones.jpg)

*Figure 3. Napier's Bones*

Invented by **John Napier**.

Used for:

- Multiplication
- Division
- Square roots

This device reduced complex mathematical calculations.

---

# 3. Pascaline (1642)

![Pascaline](/img/pascaline.jpg)

*Figure 4. Pascal Mechanical Calculator*

Invented by **Blaise Pascal**.

### Characteristics

| Feature | Value |
|---|---|
| Type | Mechanical |
| Operation | Addition & Subtraction |
| Wheels | Gear Wheels |
| Inventor | Blaise Pascal |

---

# 4. Difference Engine (1822)

![Difference Engine](/img/difference-engine.jpg)

*Figure 5. Difference Engine*

Designed by **Charles Babbage**.

Purpose:

- Mathematical tables
- Automatic calculations
- Reduced human errors

<Callout kind="tip" title="Exam Fact">
Charles Babbage is known as the **Father of Computer** because he designed the Difference Engine and Analytical Engine.
</Callout>

---

# 5. Analytical Engine (1837)

![Analytical Engine](/img/analytical-engine.jpg)

*Figure 6. Analytical Engine*

The Analytical Engine introduced the idea of a **programmable computer**.

### Main Components

| Part | Modern Equivalent |
|---|---|
| Mill | CPU |
| Store | Memory |
| Punch Card | Input |
| Printer | Output |

This architecture inspired modern computers.

---

# Ada Lovelace

![Ada Lovelace](/img/ada-lovelace.jpg)

*Figure 7. Ada Lovelace*

Ada Lovelace wrote the first algorithm for the Analytical Engine.

She is known as the **First Computer Programmer**.

---

# Harvard Mark I (1944)

![Mark I](/img/mark1.jpg)

*Figure 8. Harvard Mark I*

Developed by **Howard Aiken** with IBM.

Features:

- Electromechanical
- Automatic calculations
- Very large machine

---

# ENIAC (1946)

![ENIAC](/img/eniac.jpg)

*Figure 9. ENIAC Computer*

ENIAC stands for **Electronic Numerical Integrator and Computer**.

### Specifications

| Feature | ENIAC |
|---|---|
| Weight | 30 Tons |
| Vacuum Tubes | 18,000 |
| Speed | 5,000 additions/sec |
| Year | 1946 |

ENIAC is regarded as the first large-scale electronic digital computer.

---

# Five Generations of Computers

## First Generation (1940–1956)

![Vacuum Tube](/img/vacuum-tube.jpg)

*Figure 10. Vacuum Tube*

Technology: **Vacuum Tubes**

Examples:

- ENIAC
- UNIVAC

Advantages:

- First electronic computers

Disadvantages:

- Huge size
- High heat
- Expensive

---

## Second Generation (1956–1963)

![Transistor](/img/transistor.jpg)

*Figure 11. Transistor*

Technology: **Transistors**

Benefits:

- Smaller
- Faster
- Less heat
- More reliable

---

## Third Generation (1964–1971)

![Integrated Circuit](/img/integrated-circuit.jpg)

*Figure 12. Integrated Circuit*

Technology: **IC (Integrated Circuit)**

Features:

- High speed
- Small size
- Lower cost
- Better reliability

---

## Fourth Generation (1971–Present)

![Microprocessor](/img/microprocessor.jpg)

*Figure 13. Intel 4004 Microprocessor*

Technology: **Microprocessor**

Examples:

- Desktop
- Laptop
- Smartphone

This generation made personal computers possible.

---

## Fifth Generation (Present & Future)

![AI Computer](/img/ai-computer.jpg)

*Figure 14. Artificial Intelligence Computer*

Technology includes:

- Artificial Intelligence
- Machine Learning
- Robotics
- Natural Language Processing

Examples:

- ChatGPT
- Self-driving cars
- Smart robots

---
