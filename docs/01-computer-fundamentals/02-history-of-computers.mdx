# History of Computers

<Lede>
The history of computers is the evolution of calculating devices from the Abacus to modern AI computers.
</Lede>

![History](/img/computer-history.jpg)

## Definition

<Callout kind="def" title="Definition">
Computer history is the chronological development of computing devices from ancient manual tools to modern digital systems.
</Callout>

## Timeline

| Year | Device |
|---|---|
| 2400 BC | Abacus |
| 1617 | Napier's Bones |
| 1642 | Pascaline |
| 1822 | Difference Engine |
| 1837 | Analytical Engine |
| 1944 | Harvard Mark I |
| 1946 | ENIAC |
