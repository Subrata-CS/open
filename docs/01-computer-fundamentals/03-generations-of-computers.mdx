# Generations of Computers

<Lede>
The development of computers is divided into five generations based on the major technology used in each period. Every new generation made computers smaller, faster, cheaper, and more powerful.
</Lede>

![Timeline](/img/generation-timeline.jpg)

*Figure 1. Five Generations of Computers*

---

## What is a Computer Generation?

<Callout kind="def" title="Definition">
A computer generation is a period in the evolution of computers during which a major technological improvement changed the design, speed, size, and performance of computer systems.
</Callout>

### Timeline

| Generation | Years | Main Technology |
|---|---|---|
| First | 1940–1956 | Vacuum Tubes |
| Second | 1956–1963 | Transistors |
| Third | 1964–1971 | Integrated Circuits |
| Fourth | 1971–Present | Microprocessors |
| Fifth | Present & Future | Artificial Intelligence |

---

# First Generation (1940–1956)

![First Generation](/img/first-generation.jpg)

*Figure 2. ENIAC Computer*

The first generation used **Vacuum Tubes** as electronic switches.

![Vacuum Tube](/img/vacuum-tube.jpg)

### Characteristics

- Very large size
- High electricity consumption
- Produced excessive heat
- Machine language only
- Very expensive

### Examples

- ENIAC
- EDVAC
- UNIVAC I

### Advantages

- First electronic computers
- Faster than mechanical machines

### Disadvantages

- Huge size
- Frequent failures
- High maintenance cost

---

# Second Generation (1956–1963)

![Second Generation](/img/second-generation.jpg)

*Figure 3. IBM 7094*

The second generation replaced vacuum tubes with **Transistors**.

![Transistor](/img/transistor.jpg)

### Improvements

| Feature | Improvement |
|---|---|
| Size | Smaller |
| Speed | Faster |
| Heat | Less |
| Reliability | Better |
| Cost | Lower |

### Programming Languages

- Assembly Language
- FORTRAN
- COBOL

### Example

IBM 7094

---

# Third Generation (1964–1971)

![Third Generation](/img/third-generation.jpg)

*Figure 4. IBM System/360*

The third generation introduced **Integrated Circuits (ICs)**.

![Integrated Circuit](/img/integrated-circuit.jpg)

### Characteristics

- Smaller computers
- Higher processing speed
- Lower cost
- Multiprogramming support
- Better operating systems

### Example

IBM System/360

---

# Fourth Generation (1971–Present)

![Fourth Generation](/img/fourth-generation.jpg)

*Figure 5. Personal Computer*

The fourth generation introduced the **Microprocessor**.

![Microprocessor](/img/microprocessor.jpg)

### Features

- Personal computers
- GUI operating systems
- Internet support
- High storage capacity
- Portable devices

### Examples

- Desktop PC
- Laptop
- Smartphone
- Tablet

---

# Fifth Generation (Present & Future)

![Fifth Generation](/img/fifth-generation.jpg)

*Figure 6. Artificial Intelligence*

The fifth generation focuses on **Artificial Intelligence (AI)**.

![AI Computer](/img/ai-computer.jpg)

### Technologies

- Artificial Intelligence
- Machine Learning
- Robotics
- NLP
- Expert Systems

### Real-Life Examples

- ChatGPT
- Self-driving Cars
- Smart Robots
- Voice Assistants
