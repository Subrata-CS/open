# Computer Basics

## Overview

A **computer** is an electronic device that accepts **input**, processes data according to instructions, stores information, and produces meaningful **output**.

<img src={Hero} alt="Modern computer setup" style={{width:"100%",borderRadius:"16px"}}/>

## Why Computers Matter

Computers are essential in almost every industry because they solve problems quickly, accurately, and efficiently.

| Area | Example |
|---|---|

| Healthcare | Medical diagnosis |

| Communication | Email & Video calls |

2. Input devices send signals.

## Advantages

- High speed
- Excellent accuracy
- Large storage capacity
- Multitasking
- Global connectivity

## Limitations

- Requires electricity
- Hardware may fail
- Vulnerable to cyber attacks
- Depends on software
- Cannot think independently
