---
title: Foundations
about: The courses that teach the machine and the mathematics under everything else.
order: 1
---

- 01 | [Harvard CS50x](https://cs50.harvard.edu/x/) — the best first course in computer science that exists. Free, with real assignments.
- 02 | [Stanford CS106A — Programming Methodology](https://see.stanford.edu/Course/CS106A) — how to write programs, not just syntax.
- 03 | [MIT 6.1200J — Mathematics for Computer Science](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-1200j-mathematics-for-computer-science-spring-2024/) — proofs, counting, graphs and probability, taught for CS.
- 04 | [MIT 18.06 — Linear Algebra](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-06-linear-algebra-spring-2010/) — Gilbert Strang's course. Watch it before any machine learning.
- 05 | [MIT 18.05 — Probability and Statistics](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-05-introduction-to-probability-and-statistics-spring-2022/) — the practical introduction, Bayesian from the start.
- 06 | [MIT 18.01SC — Single Variable Calculus](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-01sc-single-variable-calculus-fall-2010/) — with full problem sets and solutions.
