- 07 | [MIT 6.006 — Introduction to Algorithms](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-006-introduction-to-algorithms-fall-2011/) — the canonical algorithms course.
- 08 | [MIT 6.004 — Computation Structures](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-004-computation-structures-spring-2009/) — from transistors up to a working processor.
- 09 | [UC Berkeley CS162 — Operating Systems](https://cs162.org/) — full lectures, notes and projects, kept current.
- 10 | [Stanford CS144 — Computer Networking](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs144/) — you build a working TCP implementation.
- 11 | [UC Berkeley CS186 — Database Systems](https://cs186berkeley.net/) — query planning and storage, not just SQL syntax.
- 12 | [MIT 6.031 — Software Construction](https://web.mit.edu/6.031/www/sp22/) — the clearest writing anywhere on specifications and testing.
