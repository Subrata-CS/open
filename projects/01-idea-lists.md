---
title: Where to find ideas
about: Curated lists to pull a project from when nothing comes to mind.
order: 1
---

- [Build Your Own X](https://github.com/codecrafters-io/build-your-own-x) — write your own git, database, shell or interpreter, step by step.
- [Project Based Learning](https://github.com/practical-tutorials/project-based-learning) — tutorials that end in a working thing, sorted by language.
- [App Ideas Collection](https://github.com/florinpop17/app-ideas) — hundreds of briefs graded from beginner to advanced.
- [The Odin Project](https://www.theodinproject.com/) — a full curriculum where every lesson ends in a project.
- [RealWorld](https://github.com/gothinkster/realworld) — the same Medium clone built in 100+ stacks, so you can compare.
